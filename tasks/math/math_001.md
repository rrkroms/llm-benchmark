# MATH-001

Solve all four problems.

1. A system processes 240 requests/minute and creates 3 database operations per request. The database safely processes 1,000 operations/minute. What percentage of capacity is consumed?

2. A retry system makes at most 4 attempts. Each attempt independently succeeds with probability 0.7. What is the probability at least one attempt succeeds?

3. An algorithm has O(n) preprocessing, O(n log n) sorting, O(n) traversal, and O(k) output. Give the overall complexity in terms of n and k.

4. A service takes 800 ms. Optimization A reduces time by 25%, then optimization B reduces the resulting time by 20%. Give final time and total percentage reduction.

Show calculations and final answers. Avoid unnecessary rounding.
